Our Mission

At Shinka, our mission is to deliver a RAM (Reliability, Availability and Maintainability), Safety assistance and Verification & Validation in terms of preparation and/or independent review of essential documentation to customer.

Your partners in RAM and Safety support

We have a highly skilled team with strong expertise in supporting customers throughout the development of rail products and projects. From the initial planning phase through to the Defects Liability Period (DLP), our team provides comprehensive assistance in the preparation and independent review of RAM (Reliability, Availability, Maintainability) and Safety deliverables, ensuring compliance, quality, and project success.
