Reliability Prediction is the cornerstone of any RAMS (Reliability, Availability, Maintainability, and Safety) program. It is a quantitative assessment performed during the design phase to estimate the Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) and the expected failure rate ($\lambda$) of your system. This analysis allows us to verify if a proposed design meets its reliability targets before a single prototype is built.
