Founder

In our SHINKA, the "founder” is typically a designated partner who sets up the firm and helps define its direction, but day‑to‑day rights and duties come from the LLP Agreement and the LLP Act, 2008.

Founder responsibilities in our SHINKA:
  1. Structure and set up the LLP, including capital contribution and key partner roles as per the LLP Agreement and law.
  2. Drive initial strategy, business model, and market positioning for sustainable growth.
  3. Build and nurture the core team, fostering a collaborative and ethical culture.
  4. Oversee major decisions and support compliance by working closely with designated partners/partner and advisors to ensure ethical and sustainable business practices.
